# Leave cover runbook

## When the owner is away

1. Open the Ops Manual for the shop (not Slack memory).
2. Daily open/close checklist is in section "Open / Close".
3. Cash reconciliation: count drawer at close; photo + note in the ledger sheet.
4. Vendor late delivery: call vendor first, then text the owner only if stock-out blocks next-day open.

## Escalation

- Do **not** invent new discount policy. Escalate to owner for anything above RM50.
- Customer complaint about food quality: offer remake once; refund needs owner approval when away.

## Passwords / systems

- POS login is in the sealed envelope in the office drawer (not in this doc).
- Wi-Fi guest password rotates monthly — see the laminated card at the counter.
